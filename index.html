Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is BidBoxx safe to use?+

Yes. Every member is KYC-verified and your funds are held in your own instant account with Bidvest Microfinance Limited, a licensed microfinance institution.

How do I qualify for a Pay Small Small loan?+

Loans go to members with a consistent savings history and no outstanding debt at other institutions. Qualified applicants are verified by a Relationship Manager, then funds are disbursed via Mobile Money over a 180-day term at 20% interest.

Can I save without joining a JointBoxx?+

Absolutely. Save Small Small works on its own — JointBoxx is optional for when you want to save with a partner, group, or institution.

What does it cost to join?+

Creating an account and starting a savings box is completely free.

What if I withdraw early?+

You can withdraw anytime, but withdrawing before your savings goal date carries a 10% early withdrawal fee.

How does JointBoxx work?+

Create a JointBoxx group to get a JBoxx Code, invite your partner, group, or institution to subscribe and contribute daily, and your admin withdraws once the target is reached.
